## Current Understanding

EML, Educational Modelling Language, is a book and learning system for writing about education through a process that demonstrates its own argument. The project is strongest when handwritten thought remains primary, AI is used as projection and structuring support, and the human returns to revise, contest, and deepen the output.

The current project does not need a new broad direction. It needs a focused writing move that converts the strongest integrated April material into a book-facing chapter seed while preserving links to source, working lanes, visual grammar, and future handwritten return.

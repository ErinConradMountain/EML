EML Project — End Goals

EML Project — End Goals Created: April 2026 Status: Living document — updated as the project advances

The Vision Produce a book that is itself a model — a four-part work that demonstrates Educational Modelling Language (EML) through its own structure, grammar, and process. The book embodies the read–write–AI loop it describes: handwriting anchors thought, AI projection externalises structure, and human re-reading trains the human model through disciplined re-entry. The purpose is to train the human, not the machine.

Guiding Principles The handwritten notebook is primary. Everything flows from embodied inscription. One page, one day. Discipline over volume. Casting, not sprinting. The book is the model. Structure is the argument. The process is the proof. Train the human, not the machine. Mastery is measured by depth of re-entry. Preserve the fingerprint. The author's voice, metaphors, and terms are non-negotiable. Grammar before content. Operations and structure precede topic and theme. Delay is a design feature. Handwriting, ingestion weekends, and cyclic return protect depth. Viability before vision. Anchor thought in workable form. MVP before grand design. Drift is inevitable; navigation is the skill. Education cultivates drift awareness. The crucible transforms; technics extends. Tools amplify what already exists — reflect first.

# EML Project — End Goals

**Created:** April 2026
**Status:** Living document — updated as the project advances

---

## The Vision

Produce a **book that is itself a model** — a four-part work that demonstrates Educational Modelling Language (EML) through its own structure, grammar, and process. The book embodies the read–write–AI loop it describes: handwriting anchors thought, AI projection externalises structure, and human re-reading trains the human model through disciplined re-entry.

The purpose is to train the human, not the machine.

### 2. The Grammar

The grammar must be formalised sufficiently that a reader can:

- **Recognise** casting, crucible, drift vectors, anchoring, and knowledge space operations in their own practice
- **Trace** a learning trajectory through knowledge space using EML's vocabulary
- **Distinguish** the three layers (Structural, Cognitive-Temporal, Agentic) in any educational encounter
- **Apply** the operations to design a learning situation, a writing process, or a human–AI interaction

| Core Operation | Target State |
|----------------|--------------|
| Casting | Formally defined with worked examples; paired with memorisation as inverse |
| The Crucible | Diagrammed as transformation engine; relationship to technique and power articulated |
| Drift Vectors | Visualised on knowledge terrain; relationship to ethical drift established |
| Anchoring | Connected to handwriting, MVP, and viable form; distinguished from mere fixity |
| Knowledge Space | Mapped as navigable terrain across multiple scales; not treated as container |
| Memorisation | Defined as compressed navigation; relationship to scaffolding and accretion clear |
| Technics | Positioned as projection infrastructure; relationship to human transcendence and its dangers |

## Guiding Principles

1. **The handwritten notebook is primary.** Everything flows from embodied inscription.
2. **One page, one day.** Discipline over volume. Casting, not sprinting.
3. **The book is the model.** Structure is the argument. The process is the proof.
4. **Train the human, not the machine.** Mastery is measured by depth of re-entry.
5. **Preserve the fingerprint.** The author's voice, metaphors, and terms are non-negotiable.
6. **Grammar before content.** Operations and structure precede topic and theme.
7. **Delay is a design feature.** Handwriting, ingestion weekends, and cyclic return protect depth.
8. **Viability before vision.** Anchor thought in workable form. MVP before grand design.
9. **Drift is inevitable; navigation is the skill.** Education cultivates drift awareness.
10. **The crucible transforms; technics extends.** Tools amplify what already exists — reflect first.

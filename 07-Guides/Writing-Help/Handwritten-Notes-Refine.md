# Handwritten Notes Refinement Guide

Purpose: keep the author's voice intact while clarifying structure.

## Core rules

- Preserve distinctive phrases and metaphors; quote them verbatim.
- Keep the original sequence unless a reorder is explicitly justified.
- Mark uncertainty and gaps rather than smoothing them away.
- Use short, exact sentences; avoid academic filler.
- Cite source pages or dates when available.

## Step 1 (Polished notes) checklist

- Date and working title at the top.
- 3-7 sentence summary before the body.
- Correct grammar without changing intent.
- Highlight new terms or phrases that feel "mine."

## Step 2 (Analytical study) checklist

- Short structural overview (what this is really about).
- Outline of the argument or pattern.
- Map to EML layers (structural / cognitive-temporal / agentic).
- Note tensions, constraints, or design questions.

## Fine-tuning moves

- Replace vague verbs with concrete actions (build, constrain, iterate, scaffold).
- Keep rhythm and cadence from the handwriting; do not flatten.
- Extract candidate definitions into `06-Lexicon/Definitions/`.

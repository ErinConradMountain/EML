# Field-Centred Study

Status: Working definition

Source: Gemini conversation pasted into Codex on 2026-05-16 and routed through the EML ChatGPT Knowledge Feed.

## Definition

Field-centred study is the study of the forces, contexts, tensions, and movements that make a concept observable. It shifts attention away from the isolated object and toward the field in which the object becomes meaningful.

## Contrast

Object-centred study asks: What is this thing?

Field-centred study asks: What field makes this thing visible, what pressure shapes it, and where is it moving?

## EML Function

In EML, field-centred study helps explain how a note draws a movement from latent space into a bounded frame. The field is created by selection and framing. Once bounded, the thought can be revisited, tested, and developed through staged return.

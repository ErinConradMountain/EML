## Working Definition

The Read-Write Model is the EML mechanism through which thought becomes visible, revisable, and trainable over time. A human inscribes thought through handwriting or another embodied mark. A tool or AI system projects structure back into view. The human then re-reads, contests, rewrites, and re-enters the transformed output. Each pass trains the human model by increasing depth of return, not by replacing the thinker.

## Core Movement

1. Inscription: the page anchors thought in human time.
2. Projection: AI or another tool externalises structure, pattern, and possible form.
3. Re-entry: the human reads the projection back into the body of thought.
4. Revision: the human accepts, rejects, sharpens, or redirects the projection.
5. Drift control: repeated return reveals whether the work is deepening, flattening, or drifting.

# Weekend Ingestion Layer

## Definition

Weekend Ingestion Layer names the dedicated temporal subsystem at the weekend that digests, condenses, and thickens the knowledge produced during the week.

The weekend switches the rhythm from daily production to listening, reflection, and low-pressure writing so that raw inputs can be metabolized into stable tokens, keywords, and scaffolds. This ingestion phase protects depth by preserving delay, allowing ideas to saturate rather than being forced into immediate output, and by reorienting the learner before the next cycle. Mode switching, write during the week and listen during the weekend, keeps agency alive; choosing reflection is as important as choosing output. The weekend also serves as system maintenance, asking what drifted, what stabilized, and what needs another visit.

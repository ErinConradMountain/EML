# Weekend as Ingestion Layer

## Summary
- The weekend is a distinct temporal subsystem whose job is ingestion, digestion, and scaffold thickening rather than new production.
- Revision time alternates writing with listening, rereading, and low-pressure reflection, which slows the cycle just enough to allow saturation and internalization.
- Tokens, keywords, and condensed structures created over the weekend feed future weeks, protecting long-term coherence and forming a layered knowledge base.

## Key Claims
1. Rhythm beats continuity: the modeled week pairs weekday inscription with weekend ingestion to resist fragmentation.
2. Ingestion is generative: condensing, reorganizing, and metabolizing ideas produces capacity for more complex work.
3. Mode switching is agentic: choosing to listen, reread, or meditate is a deliberate act that preserves energy and promotes depth.
4. Tokens signal readiness: short keywords and scaffold markers communicate when digestion has succeeded and what needs diagramming next.
5. AI must honor delay: fast automation that skips ingestion risks flattening meaning, so the protected weekend remains a design constraint.

## EML Connections
- Structural layer: scaffold thickening, tokens, and the knowledge base assembled from condensed artifacts.
- Cognitive-temporal layer: the weekly rhythm, ingestion phase, and deliberate delay that keeps learning nonlinear.
- Agentic layer: conscious choice of modes and maintenance of system legibility through revision questions.

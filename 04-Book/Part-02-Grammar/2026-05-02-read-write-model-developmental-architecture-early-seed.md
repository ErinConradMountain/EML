## Working Claim

The Read-Write Model is not only a writing workflow. It is a developmental architecture. Thought is formed through cycles of inscription, projection, return, and revision. Each pass creates a more navigable knowledge space and trains the human model through disciplined re-entry.

## Chapter Function

This chapter should formalise the Read-Write Model as one of EML's core grammatical operations. It should connect handwriting, AI projection, re-reading, and revision to developmental formation without reducing the model to a mechanical process.

## Proposed Movement

1. Handwriting as the first anchor.
2. AI projection as externalised structure.
3. Re-reading as re-entry, not passive review.
4. Revision as human judgement.
5. Drift vectors as the record of repeated movement.
6. The human model as what is trained through the loop.

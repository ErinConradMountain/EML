# Knowledge Space and Semiotics Working Section

Purpose: hold April 2026 working material about knowledge space, semiotics, markers, colour grammar, perspective, staged observation, and page-as-navigation.

This lane supports the visual and structural grammar of EML. It is distinct from the Read-Write Model lane but connected to it: read-write explains the loop of inscription and return, while knowledge-space/semiotics explains the terrain, signs, markers, and visual operations through which that loop becomes navigable.

## What Belongs Here

- Colour grammar and marker-system studies.
- Page navigation and knowledge-space terrain notes.
- Satellite perspective and staged observation outputs.
- Semiotic sequencing and developmental circuit material.
- Essays or key ideas that should later become visual-language assets.

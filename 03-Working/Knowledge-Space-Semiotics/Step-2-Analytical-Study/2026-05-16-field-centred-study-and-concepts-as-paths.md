# Field-Centred Study and Concepts as Paths

Date: 2026-05-16

Source: Gemini conversation pasted into Codex chat, routed through `09-ChatGPT-Knowledge-Feed/03-Routing-Queue/2026-05-16-gemini-share-e8b77ee22ba2-pending.md`.

## Analytical Overview

The supplied material sharpens a major EML movement: the shift from studying fixed objects to studying fields of movement. In the earlier model, a concept can be treated as an object: a quote, a definition, a note, a layer in a diagram. In the newer model, the concept is treated as a path. Its meaning depends on how it has moved, where it came from, what pressure it carries, and what future re-entry it invites.

This belongs strongly in Knowledge Space and Semiotics because the central question is no longer only "What is this idea?" but "What field makes this idea observable?" The note becomes a semiotic marker. It draws something out of latent space into a bounded region of attention.

## Outline

1. Latent space holds unbounded possibility.
2. Attention selects a movement from that latent background.
3. Framing bounds the field so the movement becomes investigable.
4. The note anchors the field as an outer inscription.
5. Staged return lets the idea fade, resurface, carry pressure, and mature.
6. The concept becomes a path with provenance, pressure, and trajectory.

## EML Layer Mapping

Structural layer:

- Field, frame, path, boundary, terrain, and context become the structural vocabulary.
- A field of study is not found fully formed; it is configured by selection and framing.

Cognitive-Temporal layer:

- Meaning appears through time, return, fading, resurfacing, and re-entry.
- The concept develops through repeated passage rather than static definition.

Agentic layer:

- The learner is not a passive observer.
- Attention performs an act of configuration, creating a workable field.
- AI dialogue can help name the field, but the human return gives it continuity.

## Implications

The phrase "a concept is a path" converts EML from a diagram of layers into a developmental grammar. It also changes the status of unfinished notes. A hazy note is not a failed object. It is a weakly bounded field that may become legible through later return.

## Tensions and Design Questions

- How can EML show the difference between a note as archive and a note as anchor?
- What visual mark should represent "field creation by selection"?
- How does the learner know when a field is too wide and must be narrowed?
- What is the difference between useful fading and simple forgetting?

Polished Body (Focused on Colour → Relationship Mapping)

Orange — Identity, Orientation, and Direction “Signatures that show identity and orientation” “Provide a perspective” “Central area of attention” “Pointing ahead / cursor” Relationship: Orange marks directional intent: where the reader should look what defines the identity of a concept how movement is guided It acts like a navigational vector, linking structure (blue) to experience. → Orange = orientation within the structure

## Project Aim

EML, Educational Modelling Language, is a book and learning system. It studies how learning becomes visible, traceable, and revisable through relationships across time. The book must become a model in book form: a hybrid of field notebook, technical manual, reflective sketchbook, and visual grammar.

## Central Mechanism

The Read-Write Model is the active engine:

1. Handwriting anchors thought through embodied delay.
2. AI projection externalises structure and possible form.
3. Human re-reading re-enters the transformed output.
4. Revision trains the human model through return.
5. Repeated passes reveal drift, depth, flattening, or emergence.

The purpose is to train the human, not the machine.

## Current May 2026 Concept Cluster

The current development thread treats a concept as a path rather than a thing at a moment. A concept becomes legible through movement, return, fading, and re-entry.

Active terms:

- Concept as Path: an idea read through provenance, pressure, trajectory, and repeated return.
- Stage Return: the process by which an inscription becomes clearer through later re-entry under changed context.
- Field-Centred Study: studying the forces, context, and bounded field that make an idea observable, rather than only studying the isolated object.
- Note as Anchor of Emergence: the note draws a movement from latent space into a field of study where it can be returned to and developed.

Use these terms carefully. Do not flatten them into generic productivity language. Preserve the developmental grammar: latent space -> selection -> bounded field -> inscription -> staged return -> concept as path -> future vector.
